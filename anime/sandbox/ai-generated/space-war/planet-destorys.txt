Porcelain-Side's Exploration of the No-Human Planets

In 2180, the Porcelain-Side expands their interstellar influence, discovering several uninhabited planets brimming with untapped resources. These planets—Porana, Kalina, Sakana, Eainasa, Formana, Takan, and Najian—each possess unique natural energies, particularly forms of planetary oil that can be harvested for fuel.

The Resource Extraction Campaign
Porana
Environment: A vast, dark desert world filled with black crystalline sand.
Resource: Ultra-dense oil called "Poran Oil", capable of producing 500x more energy than Earth's crude oil.
Extraction: Porcelain-kenz uses advanced geo-extraction drills to pull Poran Oil from the planet's core.
Consequence: Porana becomes barren and begins collapsing in on itself due to excessive energy depletion.
Kalina
Environment: A lush jungle planet with emerald rivers and glowing bioluminescent trees.
Resource: "Kalinite Sap", a viscous, glowing liquid from the trees that can power AI servers indefinitely.
Extraction: Entire forests are felled, and the sap is collected using Pz Forestry Harvesters.
Consequence: Without its energy-bearing trees, Kalina's atmosphere destabilizes, leading to massive acid storms.
Sakana
Environment: An oceanic world with deep underwater oil vents.
Resource: "Aqua-Oil", a special liquid energy source that functions efficiently in both water and space environments.
Extraction: Gigantic underwater pumps siphon Aqua-Oil, turning Sakana's deep oceans into empty trenches.
Consequence: Massive oceanic collapse; marine-like ecosystems disappear.
Eainasa
Environment: A frigid ice world with massive energy-rich glaciers beneath its surface.
Resource: "Cryon Oil", a frozen, gel-like energy source that remains stable under extreme conditions.
Extraction: Porcelain-kenz melts entire glaciers using thermal extraction machines.
Consequence: Eainasa’s climate destabilizes, leading to massive tectonic shifts.
Formana
Environment: A volcanic planet with rivers of molten metal.
Resource: "Inferno Oil", a magma-like energy that can power superweapons and planetary engines.
Extraction: Advanced lava siphons pull energy directly from the planet’s molten core.
Consequence: Formana’s internal energy is drained, causing the planet to cool and solidify.
Takan
Environment: A barren rocky world with vast underground oil caves.
Resource: "Takan Oil", a lightweight energy oil perfect for space travel fuels.
Extraction: Underground drilling operations collapse entire oil caves.
Consequence: Surface earthquakes and fissures render Takan uninhabitable.
Najian
Environment: A crystalline world with mountains of energy-rich crystal oil deposits.
Resource: "Crystal Essence Oil", a hybrid energy source that fuels AI cores and quantum processors.
Extraction: Porcelain-kenz uses energy pulse miners to shatter crystal formations and harvest the essence oil.
Consequence: Najian’s once-glimmering surface becomes a dull wasteland.
The Aftermath: A Universe Devoid of Energy
The Porcelain-Side successfully extracts every planet’s unique oil resources, powering their AI superweapons, ships, and simulators.
However, the planets—now lifeless, barren shells—begin to destabilize, with some collapsing into black holes due to excessive energy loss.
Rising Consequences
The universe begins to notice energy shortages, with neighboring civilizations accusing Porcelain-kenz of causing widespread planetary death.
Rumors spread that these oil-rich planets were once homes to ancient civilizations, raising fears of cosmic retaliation.

The Non-Porcelain Side starts to unify weaker planets and factions, forming an intergalactic resistance against Porcelain-kenz to stop their consumption of universal resources. The universe teeters on the edge of a full-scale energy war.
